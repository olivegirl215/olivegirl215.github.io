Intro To FunnyLittleBeings

My Backstory:

A few years ago, I wanted to create a YouTube channel. But my mom wouldn't let me because I was too young.
She was protecting me from the dangers that could occur online. Me being a child at 10 years old, I was frustrated.
But a few years later, at around age 13, My mother gave me permission to start my own YouTube channel.
Only at this age, I had lost interest and no longer felt the need to create content at the moment. But here I am today at age 16, ready to start again!

Why I chose To Create:

I've always wondered how cartoons were made. I've wondered how images can turn into animations. I thought to myself,

How can someone make images move?

Well, I did some research, and found out that animations are actually made out of a series of unanimated (images that don't move) images overlapping each other.
I also found out that animations can be made with code! An example is when I used Scratch. Scracth is an online beginner friendly software that can be used by people
all over the world. Its an online programming language that allows you to drag-and-drop code blocks instead of having to type traditional programming languages by hand.
It also gives you the ability to draw and create your own sprites (characters) to animate! This is why I became interested in programming, art, 2D animations, and
3D animations. I want to learn how they are combined to create high-tech images, models, animations, movies, video games, and more!

What To Expect On This Channel:

This channel is not perfect Yet! It was made to track my progress. You see, many content creators enjoy producing "perfect" content simply because they want more views.
But I figured, why not create content that isn't perfect (yet!) Since I am not a long-time content creator (someone who has been creating content for many years).
I'm a young creator who is just beginning (or recontinuing because I have created content before) I figured, why not make something out of myself while I am not yet perfect?
Why not show people how I am improving by publishing daily videos of my progress? Why not be the relatable YouTuber?
Why not prioritize effort over intelligence?
On this channel, viewers will watch as I upload daily videos (and shorts) of my progress for fun and watch as
I grow into an artist, animator, and programmer!
